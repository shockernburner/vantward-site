Hushclaw

Stay quiet. Stay close. Leave no one.

In some parallel universe, the dinosaurs never died out. Hushclaw is a first-person co-op survival game: drop onto a new island every match, recover what was left at dead outposts, and get everyone to the helicopter.

Play on itch.io Windows · macOS · Name your own price

How a match works

  1. Drop in. Every match is a new procedurally generated island of jungle, plains, swamps, rivers and ruins.
  2. Recover. Search dead outposts. Black boxes beep, and the beeping draws dinosaurs to you.
  3. Deliver. Bring what you found to the landing pad and fire a flare.
  4. Hold. Survive 45 seconds until the helicopter arrives. Teammates who go down can be revived.

The island

Dinosaurs that listen

They hear you, track your scent, growl before they charge, pounce, swing their tails and knock you down.

Night and weather

Night matches with night vision goggles, plus storms, fog and rain.

The Field Archive

Recovered items are sealed files that hold a contract, a story page, a perk or an outfit.

Tools to stay alive

Heal, throw a noise lure, light a hand flare, and shop between fights.

Play together

Co-op for up to four

Join with a code and talk through proximity voice chat.

Daily Island and Weekly Contract

Shared by every player, with a daily streak.

Leaderboards

Worldwide boards for survivors and for teams, with custom team emblems.

Fair by design

In-match purchases use in-match coins only. Nothing is sold for power.

Questions

Can I play Hushclaw now?

Yes. The alpha (version 0.3.0) is on itch.io for Windows and macOS, and you name your own price.

How many people can play together?

Play solo, or co-op with up to four players using a join code. Teammates hear each other through proximity voice chat.

Can I play solo?

Yes. Solo has Easy, Medium and Hard difficulty. Co-op always plays on Hard.

Is it pay-to-win?

No. Everything you buy during a match is paid for with coins earned in that match, and nothing is sold for power.

Is Hushclaw the game that was called TETHER: Primal?

Yes. Hushclaw is the new name of the co-op dinosaur survival game we first announced as TETHER: Primal.

Who makes Hushclaw?
